## Observations

- The tutorial mission is balanced for a first-time player: battle-heavy with generous gold.
- Act 1 hard gives less starting gold but amplifies earnings, rewarding skilled play.
- Ascension difficulty is punishing: 60 start gold and the scarcest recruitment pools.
- Workshop prices (150–220 gold) are reachable mid-expedition but force trade-offs against merchant purchases and recruitment.
- Anchor nodes provide the only guaranteed instability reduction; altars trade 10 instability for boons.
